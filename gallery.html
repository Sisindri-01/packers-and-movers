Types of Vehicles Available

We own a diverse fleet ranging from nimble pickup trucks to heavy 32ft MX container trucks to suit your exact volume.

Mini Trucks (Tata Ace)

Perfect for local 1BHK shifting or moving a small number of items quickly.

Container Trucks (Eicher)

Fully enclosed containers to protect your goods during 2-3 BHK long-distance moves.

Massive high-capacity trucks specifically for large corporate and industrial relocations.
