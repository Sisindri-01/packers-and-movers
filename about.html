Our Story

India's Most Trusted Logistics Partner

Started in 2011 with just two trucks and a vision, Surya Packers and Movers has grown into a nationwide network of logistics experts. We realized early on that moving isn't just about transporting boxes; it's about moving memories, careers, and lives.

Today, we operate a massive fleet of custom-built carriers, utilizing state-of-the-art tracking technology and premium packing materials. Whether you are moving across the street or across the country, we guarantee a seamless, damage-free experience.
