Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does shifting cost?
The cost depends on various factors like the volume of goods, distance between locations, packing materials required, and floor level. We offer free surveys to provide an accurate and affordable quote.
Do you provide transit insurance?
Yes, we provide comprehensive transit insurance for all your belongings. In the rare event of damage or loss during transit, the insurance cover will compensate you appropriately.
How long does relocation take?
Local shifting within the city is usually completed in a single day. Interstate moves depend on the distance, but typically range from 3 to 7 working days. We provide estimated timelines during booking.
Is packing material cost included in the quote?
Yes, our formal quotations are all-inclusive. It covers labor, transportation, loading/unloading, and all premium packing materials required for safe transit.
Do you transport bikes/cars?
Absolutely. We have specialized, enclosed vehicle carriers specifically designed to transport cars and motorcycles safely without any wear and tear.
